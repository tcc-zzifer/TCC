# TCC Cosmology — The Circular Architecture

## 1. The System of Circle Absorption
Unlike flat pyramids or disconnected dimensional "layers," the cosmology of **TCC (The Circular Canon)** is built upon the **System of Circle Absorption**—a continuous, nested architecture where every smaller circle is wholly encompassed within a larger one, forming a unified macro-structure.

* **Structural Inclusion:** Any smaller circle—regardless of its internal scale, number of universes, dimensional depth, or complexity—exists merely as an internal component of a larger encompassing circle.
* **Formation of the Macro-Structure:** Absorption functions as structural containment rather than chaotic erasure. Each outer circle encloses the entirety of the inner circles within its boundary, binding them together as nodes of a single coherent design.
* **Asymmetry of Scope:** Entities and laws bound to an inner circle operate strictly within its perimeter; to the encompassing outer circle, that entire reality and all of its internal hierarchies represent just a single structural element.
* **Position of World 0:** Even the foundational plane of **World 0** (inherently 4D in nature) serves only as one of the inner circles within this vast nested hierarchy, surrounded by progressively greater encompassing circles up to the boundary of the Demiurge Spectrum.
