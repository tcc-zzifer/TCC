## Overview
**TCC** is a logical analytical framework and fictional cosmology designed to evaluate scale, combat, and worldbuilding through visual proof, rejecting narrative inflation and arbitrary tiers.

## Core Principles
1. **Visual Primacy & Logic:** Tiers reflect proven scale; visual feats override unbacked text.
2. **Vector Validation:** Stats like Striking Force, Speed, Durability, and Creation are measured separately with explicit costs and tempos.
3. **Law of Price & Dual Modes:** Abilities require stamina or resource costs. Engagements split into **Equal** (baseline) and **Situational** (tactical) modes.

## The 16-Tier Scale
* **1–6:** Sub-Biological to City & Metropolis.
* **7–12:** Mountain/Island to Galactic & Cluster.
* **13–14:** Universal & Multiversal.
* **15–16:** Dimensional (4D–6D+) & Demiurge Spectrum (Local, Bound, Autonomous).
