## Overview
**TCC (The Circular Canon)** is an independent fictional cosmology and a pure-logic analytical framework designed for worldbuilders, writers, and objective analysts. It calibrates scale, mechanics, and combat interactions through strict visual verification and internal consistency—eliminating narrative inflation, arbitrary dimensional labels, and contradictory scaling chains.

## Fundamental Axioms
1. **Pure Logic Rules Here:** A tier measures the confirmed scale of a specific capability, never an automatic guarantee of victory.
2. **Visual Primacy:** Completed visual execution on the primary canvas overrides unbacked statements, metaphors, and non-visual lore.
3. **Mandatory Vector Validation:** Aggregate single-number tiers are prohibited; every tier is tagged to a specific attribute (**Striking Force**, **Speed**, **Durability**, or **Creation**) with verified tempo and resource cost.
4. **The Law of Price & Attrition:** Every ability requires stamina, activation time, resources, or irreversible cost. Internal anatomy does not scale to external armor plating.
5. **Dual Engagement Modes:** **Equal Engagement** (innate baseline parameters) vs. **Situational Engagement** (preparation, terrain, traps, and external gear).
