01 // Core Analytical Standard

TCC (The Circular Canon) is an independent tactical combat and cosmology framework built specifically for the Team Fortress 2 and Source Engine community. External scaling wikis (VSBW, CSAP) and "Boundless / Tier 0" paradoxes are strictly banned.

  • Visual Primacy & Gameplay Segregation: Feats are verified strictly through official visual media (Valve comics, Meet the Team shorts, scripted cutscenes, and canonical SFM works), separating narrative reality from in-game UI (HP bars, respawns, cooldowns).
  • Mandatory Vector Tagging: Striking Force, Combat Speed, Reaction Speed, Travel Velocity, and 4-layer Durability are calibrated independently—never merged into one inflated rank.
  • Law of Price & Latency: Every ability incurs stamina drain, structural recoil, activation wind-up, or recovery windows.
  • Supremacy of Battle Intelligence (BIQ): Tactical positioning, blind spots, psychology, and environmental traps decide street-level and mid-tier Source engagements.
