# TCC Standard Dossier: Scale Tiers & Vector Validation Protocol

## 1. Fundamental Conditions for Tier Assignment
Within the **TCC (The Circular Canon)** analytical standard, assigning a single aggregate rank to an entity is strictly prohibited. Any tier from the scale below is valid only when three mandatory criteria are met:

* **Direct On-Canvas Execution:** A tier is credited exclusively for a completed, direct feat performed by the entity itself (direct destruction, creation, total containment/control, or direct damage endurance). Indirect destruction, traps, and environmental chain reactions are excluded from the baseline profile and evaluated separately under the **Situational Protocol**.
* **Tempo and Cost Documentation:** When a tier is verified via **Creation**, the exact timeframe and resource cost of the act must be explicitly recorded (instantaneous combat deployment versus an extended preparatory cycle). Out-of-combat creation does not equate to equivalent melee or offensive striking output.
* **Mandatory Vector Tagging:** A tier applies strictly to the specific verified parameter without carrying over to unconfirmed attributes (e.g., *Tier 6 — Energy Output / Tier 2 — Internal Durability*).

---

## 2. Complete 16-Tier Scale of TCC
* **Tier 1 — Sub-Biological:** Microorganisms, insects, and objects falling below the threshold of baseline human anatomy.
* **Tier 2 — Baseline Human:** Average humans, standard fauna, and conventional melee or small-arms weaponry.
* **Tier 3 — Peak Human & Tactical:** The physiological limit of a conditioned human body, tactical unit equipment, and the capacity to breach brick walls, doors, or light vehicles.
* **Tier 4 — Building:** Structural collapse of standalone buildings, reinforced bunkers, and heavy armored vehicles.
* **Tier 5 — District & Sector:** Neutralization or destruction of building clusters, industrial complexes, or major urban districts.
* **Tier 6 — City & Metropolis:** Destruction or complete defense of an entire metropolis within a single bounded combat cycle.
* **Tier 7 — Mountain & Island:** Destruction of major geographical formations, including mountain ranges, volcanoes, and islands.
* **Tier 8 — Continental:** Tectonic-scale impact affecting oceanic plates and entire continents without fracturing the host planet.
* **Tier 9 — Planetary:** Overcoming gravitational binding energy to displace or destroy moons, terrestrial planets, and gas giants in their entirety.
* **Tier 10 — Stellar:** Creation, extinction, or destruction of individual stars and compact binary star systems.
* **Tier 11 — Solar System:** Complete destruction or structural reconfiguration of an entire planetary-stellar system, including orbital space and outer belts.
* **Tier 12 — Galactic & Cluster:** Large-scale impact encompassing entire galaxies, supermassive black holes, and—at the upper boundary—galaxy clusters and superclusters within a single physical continuum.
* **Tier 13 — Universal:** Total creation, structural reconfiguration, or collapse of a single closed universe (a continuous spacetime continuum).
* **Tier 14 — Multiversal:** Direct control over or impact upon multiple autonomous universes, parallel timelines, or nested concentric circles operating on a single baseline dimensional plane.
* **Tier 15 — Dimensional:** Existence and operation on spatial planes above the baseline three-dimensional layer (4D and above), confirmed by qualitative superiority over lower structures. Within its own plane, the entity remains bounded by form, internal laws, and vulnerability to defeat. Divided into two subcategories:
  * **15-A. Low Dimensional:** Primary higher dimensions (4D–5D), allowing the entity to perceive and manipulate lower universes and multiverses as flat, enclosed objects.
  * **15-B. High Dimensional:** Deep multidimensional planes and complex dimensional hierarchies (6D and above) possessing verified qualitative superiority over baseline dimensional layers.
* **Tier 16 — Demiurge Spectrum:** The upper boundary for active entities within a fictional work, divided into three ranks based on structural freedom and the absence of vulnerabilities:
  * **16-A. Local Demiurge:** Governs and controls only its own closed circle or isolated dimension, remaining unaware of higher overarching layers.
  * **16-B. Bound Demiurge:** Creator of a major cosmology constrained by narrative vulnerabilities, fragmentation into aspects, dormant states, or dependency on its host world.
  * **16-C. Autonomous Demiurge:** An indivisible, conscious architect exercising unilateral control over the entire cosmology without internal anchors, dependencies, or points of failure.

*(Note: The real-world author and the out-of-narrative domain remain strictly outside the scale and are excluded from analytical evaluation.)*

# TCC Standard Dossier: Non-Visual Media Exclusion & Analytical Precision

Within the TCC (The Circular Canon) framework, purely textual and audio-only formats (novels, light novels, unillustrated lore entries, radio dramas, and audiobooks) are strictly excluded from structural evaluation and tier assignment. This restriction is enforced to eliminate analytical distortion caused by subjective interpretation.
• The Subjectivity Gap: Written prose is inherently superficial and abstract. A single sentence describing a "world-shattering strike" or "blinding speed" generates wildly different mental images across different readers, making objective calibration impossible.
• Absence of Spatial Anchors: Without a visual frame, analysts cannot verify obstacle density, exact distances, environmental scale, or anatomical recoil. Evaluating text forces reliance on guesswork rather than empirical observation.
• Minimizing Analytical Error: Visual media already requires careful contextual scrutiny to avoid misinterpretation. Eliminating non-visual sources removes the primary vector of unresolvable translation disputes, poetic ambiguity, and reader projection.

2. Classification of Accompanying Text & Verified Potential

Written dialogue, captions, and narration appearing inside visual media (manga panels, comic boxes, or animated subtitles) are strictly subordinate to the visual frame and categorized into three distinct analytical tiers:

Verified Potential is recorded as a separate conditional tag—never merged into confirmed baseline durability or single-strike output—and depends on situational escalation.
• Unbacked Statement (Narrative Dialogue): Claims, boasts, or character assumptions lacking direct visual corroboration. If words do not match the visual reality on screen, they are classified strictly as dramatic dialogue, subjective guesswork, or hyperbole and carry zero analytical weight.
• Contextual Verification Mandate: Analysts must cross-examine every explanatory caption against the visual action. Whether a statement constitutes valid mechanical clarification or mere character speculation is determined case-by-case based on visual evidence and situational context.

3. Scope of Valid Media Formats

To qualify for TCC tiering and combat evaluation, a work must provide direct, continuous visual data of its entities and environments:
