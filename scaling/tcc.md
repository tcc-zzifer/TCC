# TCC SCALING — Introduction & Author's Note

> **Notice:** This scaling system is not a universal standard built for the public, nor does it claim to be flawless. It is a deliberate, author-driven attempt to patch the recurring logical loopholes found across conventional powerscaling—crafted specifically for the **TCC** universe.

---

### Author's Vision & Purpose

**TCC Scaling** represents the author's personal vision of how hierarchy, scale, and power should function within fiction. It does not pretend to be the "best" or absolute truth in logic, yet it actively works to eliminate contradictions, hollow statements, and broken tiers that plague traditional systems.

Whether you choose to accept this logic or reject it is entirely your decision. You are free to criticize it, tear it apart, or praise it—it makes no difference to its foundation. **This is my structure**, built according to my own reasoning rather than for mass approval or community consensus.

### Built Specifically for the TCC Cosmology

* **Tailored to One World:** While the principles outlined here *can* be applied to other works of fiction, **this system is created specifically for the TCC cosmology**, which itself is strictly grounded in internal logic.
* **Cosmology & Scaling as One:** Here, scaling is not detached from world-building—every tier exists to serve and align with the structural rules, layers, and mechanics of **TCC** without leaving gaps behind.

---

## Tier Hierarchy & Scale Structure

Instead of relying on generic wiki templates, **TCC Scaling** divides power and scale into sequential **Scales**, where each Scale is broken down into four distinct, grounded progression tiers *(with the sole exception of Demiurges, which follow their own classification)*:
* **Starting level** — the entry threshold of a given scale.
* **Average level** — the standard, established baseline of that scale.
* **Large level** — the upper threshold before a qualitative leap in threat or capability.
* **Extra-large level** — the peak limit of the current scale, bridging the gap to the next tier.


---

### 1 Scale (Sub-Human & Biological Baseline)

* **1-Starting level** — Microorganisms, single-celled organisms, and protozoan parasites.
* **1-Average level** — Insects and small animals.
* **1-Large level** — Weakened individuals, disabled persons, children, incapable individuals, cats, corgis, and similar small domestic animals.
* **1-Extra-large level** — Wolves, wild boars, lynxes, and comparable predatory or heavy wildlife.

### 2 Scale — Human Level
* **2-Starting level** — Ordinary adult civilians without combat training, athletic conditioning, or real fight experience.
* **2-Average level** — Physically fit adults, standard soldiers, street fighters, or individuals armed with basic melee or standard firearms.
* **2-Large level** — Professional athletes, combat veterans, elite special forces operatives, and masters of close-quarters combat and tactical marksmanship within real human anatomy.
* **2-Extra-large level** — The absolute peak of real human biology (maximum strength, endurance, reaction speed, and high BIQ without supernatural traits) paired with light weaponry.

### 3 Scale — Superhuman & Tactical Level
* **3-Starting level** — Initial breach beyond human anatomy: shattering wood, brick, and thin metal, and surviving impacts lethal to ordinary humans.
* **3-Average level** — Destroying load-bearing walls, concrete slabs, and bending thick steel; equivalent to heavy-caliber firearms and fragmentation grenades.
* **3-Large level** — Demolishing structural supports, fortified rooms, and light armored vehicles; equivalent to heavy infantry ordnance (RPGs, autocannons).
* **3-Extra-large level** — Instant obliteration of large fortified interiors, tank armor, and heavy barriers without yet reaching full-building scale.

### 4 Scale — Building & Complex Scale
* **4-Starting level** — Complete destruction or collapse of small structures: houses, bunkers, hangars, and isolated outposts.
* **4-Average level** — Destruction of standard multi-story residential buildings, administrative blocks, and industrial halls.
* **4-Large level** — Demolition of massive high-rises, skyscrapers, and major military bases.
* **4-Extra-large level** — Total annihilation of gigantic multi-level complexes and heavy fortification hubs in a single decisive act.

### 5 Scale — District & Sector Scale
* **5-Starting level** — Destruction of a single dense city block or a fortified defensive cluster of multiple buildings.
* **5-Average level** — Leveling several consecutive city blocks or a large residential/industrial district.
* **5-Large level** — Destruction of an entire urban sector or major industrial zone.
* **5-Extra-large level** — Complete erasure of a small town or large military sector on the threshold of a full city.

### 6 Scale — City & Terrain Scale
* **6-Starting level** — Destruction of a full small city along with its entire infrastructure.
* **6-Average level** — Destruction of a major city or large urban agglomeration.
* **6-Large level** — Leveling gigantic metropolises and deep multi-tiered subterranean cities.
* **6-Extra-large level** — Shattering or displacing entire mountain ranges, ridges, and major geological formations.

### 7 Scale — Island & Nation Scale
* **7-Starting level** — Destruction or splitting of a large island.
* **7-Average level** — Scale of an archipelago or the territory of a small nation.
* **7-Large level** — Destruction of the territory of a full medium-sized country.
* **7-Extra-large level** — Destruction of vast territories comparable to the largest sovereign powers.

### 8 Scale — Continental Scale
* **8-Starting level** — Critical devastation of a major region or subcontinent.
* **8-Average level** — Complete destruction or displacement of an entire continent.
* **8-Large level** — Simultaneous destruction of multiple continents.
* **8-Extra-large level** — Total scorching of a planet's entire surface, biosphere, and crust without shattering its gravitational core.

### 9 Scale — Planetary Scale
* **9-Starting level** — Destruction of large asteroids, moons, and dwarf planets.
* **9-Average level** — Complete destruction of a standard Earth-like planet, overcoming its gravitational binding integrity.
* **9-Large level** — Destruction of super-Earths and ice giants.
* **9-Extra-large level** — Destruction of gas giants on the scale of Jupiter up to the threshold of stellar ignition.

### 10 Scale — Stellar Scale
* **10-Starting level** — Destruction of brown, red, and white dwarfs.
* **10-Average level** — Complete destruction of full main-sequence stars of solar type.
* **10-Large level** — Destruction of massive stars and blue/red giants.
* **10-Extra-large level** — Destruction of supergiants and hypergiants at the upper limit of stellar mass.

### 11 Scale — Solar System Scale
* **11-Starting level** — Destruction of a star along with its inner planetary orbits.
* **11-Average level** — Complete destruction of a local star system with all its planets and belts within a unified radius.
* **11-Large level** — Scale of extended binary and multiple-star systems.
* **11-Extra-large level** — Simultaneous destruction of dozens or hundreds of neighboring star systems (interstellar sector).

### 12 Scale — Galactic Scale
* **12-Starting level** — Destruction of dense star clusters and dwarf galaxies.
* **12-Average level** — Complete destruction or creation of a full spiral galaxy on the scale of the Milky Way.
* **12-Large level** — Destruction of giant elliptical galaxies and local galaxy groups.
* **12-Extra-large level** — Destruction of galaxy superclusters and large-scale cosmic filaments up to the boundaries of observable matter.

### 13 Scale — Universal Scale
* **13-Starting level** — Destruction of all observable matter within a universe without affecting its underlying space and time.
* **13-Average level** — Complete destruction or creation of a single closed universe (3D space together with its linear timeline).
* **13-Large level** — Destruction of a large composite universe encompassing attached pocket dimensions or internal sub-layers within a single timeline.
* **13-Extra-large level** — Complete erasure of an entire universal continuum with all its internal realms on the threshold of multiple independent universes.

### 14 Scale — Multiversal Scale
* **14-Starting level** — A small group of separate, independent universes not yet bound into a unified macro-system.
* **14-Average level** — A full multiverse: a large, cohesive network or system of numerous independent universes within a single plane of existence.
* **14-Large level** — An endless or infinite multiverse comprising countless independent universes across one horizontal layer.
* **14-Extra-large level** — The ceiling of a multiversal plane: the totality of universes and structural links within a single dimensional tier prior to ascending to a higher dimensional layer (15 Scale).

* ### 15 Scale — High Dimensional
* **15-Lower (3–7D)** — Lower higher dimensions: breaching beyond standard 3D space into the 3D+ to 7D range.
* **15-Middle (7–11D)** — Middle higher dimensions: entities and structures operating within the 7D to 11D range.
* **15-Higher (11D+)** — Upper higher dimensions: the 11D+ range, marking the final dimensional threshold prior to Demiurges (Tier 16).

### 16 — The Demiurge Spectrum (Author's Ceiling of TCC)
> **Structural Exception:** Demiurges are not subdivided into `Starting`, `Average`, `Large`, or `Extra-large`. Their standing is determined not by raw destructive output, but by **independence from creation, absence of vulnerabilities, non-descent into the world, and the architectural quality of the cosmos itself.**

#### 16-A — Local Demiurge
* **Nature & Scope:** A creator whose authority and creative nature are confined within a single local world, isolated universe, pocket realm, or bounded sector of reality.
* **Constraints:** Beyond its native domain or against higher cosmological layers, its creative supremacy ceases to apply. It remains bound by internal laws, finite reserves, emotional limits, or direct defeat by comparable forces, and actively interacts with its creation.

### 16-B — Bound Demiurge
*(Subdivided into two tiers based on the degree of attachment to creation)*

* **16-B1 — Descending / Dependent Demiurge:**
  * The creator of a vast, multi-layered cosmos (including higher dimensions) that retains direct ties to its creation.
  * Possesses confirmed vulnerabilities (can be harmed, weakened, deceived, or disrupted), depends on the state, laws, or balance of the world, and descends into creation directly or through avatars.
* **16-B2 — Non-Contacting Limited Demiurge:**
  * A transitional tier just below the apex. It stands above the cosmic architecture and refrains from interfering in mortal affairs, yet fails one of the strict conditions of absolute autonomy—possessing a canonical vulnerability, leaving traceable anchors inside creation, or presiding over a small, flawed, or logically inconsistent world.

### 16-C — Autonomous Demiurge / Creator-Observer (Absolute Ceiling)
* **Nature & Scope:** The supreme, most coherent formulation of a creator within fiction. It rejects the self-contradictory label of *"Boundless"*—as anything written exists within the boundary of the work—yet within that boundary, its supremacy is total and unblemished.
* **Five Mandatory Conditions of 16-C:**
  1. **Zero Vulnerabilities:** No actual or depicted weaknesses exist. It cannot be reached, weakened, deceived, or tied to any law, state, or event inside creation.
  2. **Non-Existence of Creation:** To a 16-C entity, its world does not exist as tangible reality—it is merely a drawn abstraction observed from outside the glass.
  3. **Absolute Non-Descent:** It never steps down into the world, deploys no avatars, leaves no traces, and issues no commandments.
  4. **Total Unawareness of Creation:** Inhabitants of the world lack the very senses, concepts, or mechanisms required to ever know or suspect its presence.
  5. **Architectural Perfection:** Supreme standing must be earned through the caliber of the design—the created cosmos must be vast, intricate, multi-layered, and free of logical rot.

## TCC 4.0 Analytical Standard

### 1. Visual Primacy (`Feat >>>>>> Statement`)
* Only **visual media** (animation, film, scripted video game cutscenes, comics, manga) are valid for evaluation. Purely textual novels, audio narratives, and unillustrated lore entries are excluded.
* **The Visual Frame is Absolute:** Character dialogue, narration, databooks, and external creator interviews carry zero analytical weight unless corroborated by completed on-screen execution.
* **No Speculative Inflation:** Claims of "holding back" or unshown potential never grant higher scales; profiles are locked strictly to demonstrated ceilings.

### 2. Ban on Pixel-Scaling & Reverse Energy Math
* Measuring screen pixels and converting flashes, dust clouds, or shockwaves into joules or tons of TNT is **strictly prohibited**. Art serves composition, not engineering blueprints.
* Destruction (`1–14 Scale`) is calibrated **literally by the structural object breached or displaced**, its physical material, density, and thickness—never by decorative visual effects or abstract room labels.
* Collapsing a building by severing weak pillars or bypassing walls with acid/phasing reflects tactical precision or specialized mechanics, not baseline building-level striking force.

### 3. The Law of Price, Stamina & Anatomy
* Combatants are functional biological or mechanical systems, not infinite spreadsheets. Every action is bound by stamina limits, thermal strain, pain shock, and post-attack recovery (**The Law of Price**).
* **No Monolithic Durability:** External armor does not protect internal organs from shockwaves, asphyxiation, toxins, radiation, or precision strikes to unarmored weak points (eyes, joints, sensors).
* Regeneration is strictly bounded by energy/biomass reserves and critical structural anchor points (brain, heart, core).

### 4. Supremacy of Battle Intelligence (BIQ) & Non-Linear Outcomes
* Holding a higher `Scale` measures capacity, **never a guaranteed victory**. Outcomes depend on BIQ, starting range, terrain, execution errors under stress, and style compatibility.
* **No `A > B > C` Chains:** Defeating a superior opponent via traps, weak points, or exhaustion does not grant the victor that opponent's physical scale.
* Aim-dodging reflects BIQ and anticipation, not super-speed. Teleportation is spatial utility, not physical combat velocity.
* Every evaluation must declare its mode upfront: **Equal Engagement** (unassisted baseline, no prep) or **Situational Engagement** (traps, prep time, specialized gear).

### 5. Separation of Speed Vectors & Specialized Mechanics
* Velocity is divided into three non-interchangeable metrics: **Combat Speed**, **Travel Velocity**, and **Reaction Speed**.
* Specialized abilities (Hax, illusions, mind/soul manipulation, spatial warping) are bounded by demonstrated radius, activation latency, stamina cost, and target compatibility (e.g., mind attacks fail against soulless machines).
* **No Artificial Verse Equalization:** Incompatible energy systems (stamina, radiation, spiritual energy, tech cores) are never merged into a single universal fuel.

### 6. Segregation of Gags, Gameplay & Non-Canon
* Comedic gags, parodies, and slapstick anomalies are isolated in a zero-evidence zone and **never override an entity's serious narrative baseline**.
* Interactive games are evaluated **strictly through scripted story cutscenes**, never through UI health bars, respawns, or gameplay exploits.
* Distinct visual adaptations (anime, comics, films, games) and chronological eras are evaluated separately—**zero artificial composites** and zero cross-scaling via background Easter eggs.

### 7. Unfiltered Canon & Psychological Integrity
* Canonical defeats, operational mistakes, aging, and permanent injuries are mandatory parts of an entity's profile and cannot be erased to protect an inflated rating.
* Combatants retain their canonical personality, moral limiters, and arrogance. Protagonist plot armor is stripped away in neutral evaluations.
* Identical evidentiary standards apply symmetrically to every entity without exception.

### 8. Digital Simulations & Subordinate Units
* Manipulating code or deleting files inside a software sandbox applies **strictly within that host program** and holds zero equivalence to destroying physical matter (`13 Scale`). Outside the network, digital entities remain bound to physical hardware.
* Summoned creatures, drones, and armies are evaluated separately from their commander; neutralizing the commander concludes the engagement.

### 9. Validation of 13–15 Scale (Universes & Higher Dimensions)
* Labels like "dimension," "realm," or "pocket universe" carry zero weight without visualized volume: a city-sized pocket realm is **6 Scale**, not **13 Scale**.
* Observing a single universe from an external interface or pocket space does not grant multiversal standing (**14 Scale**).
* **15 Scale (`High Dimensional`)** requires explicit structural proof of existing on or operating within a higher dimension (`3–7D`, `7–11D`, `11D+`), never poetic buzzwords.

### 10. Abolition of "Boundless" & Out-of-Scale Author Boundary (Tier 16)
* The self-contradictory label **"Boundless" is permanently banned**: anything defined within a catalog exists inside the boundary of fiction.
* The absolute ceiling inside fiction is **The Demiurge Spectrum (`16-A` / `16-B1` / `16-B2` / `16-C`)**, where supreme **16-C** standing demands zero vulnerabilities, absolute non-descent, and a vast, logically coherent cosmology.
* The real-world human author stands **strictly outside the scale** and is excluded from evaluation.

### 11. The Law of Price & Impact Classification
* **Consequence of Action:** Every deployment of force carries a mandatory cost—ranging from stamina drain and structural recoil to psychological strain and moral consequences within the narrative.
* **Three Tiers of Structural Impact:**
  * **Fracture / Breach:** Piercing or cracking a target without destroying its overall structural mass.
  * **Destruction:** Shattering or collapsing a target into debris.
  * **Total Erasure:** Complete annihilation of matter or existence without remainder.
